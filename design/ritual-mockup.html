WidgetKit · desktop + Notification Center

Widgets and menu bar

The widgets follow the active skin. Orb and Today list are interactive: you can check a habit off straight from the desktop. The menu bar shows today's count, and clicking it opens a quick-check popover.

Stats

The year field and the climb

The year field puts each month in a column and each day on a row. A done day becomes a small shard, and a missed day stays a quiet dot with no red. The climb chart plots all 60 reps as a switchback path up a low-poly mountain, with a flag at each of the 8 levels.

Climb
Journey · 60 reps · 8 levels

Every rep places a piece

Each level builds a small low-poly monument, and every check-in adds one block. The real app generates these in code with RealityKit, so no hand-modelled 3D assets are needed. Missed days never remove a piece.

    Skin tokens

    Five worlds to start

    A skin swaps the background, the orb material, the check colors, the ink and one accent. Every screen and widget reads from these same tokens.

    The mockup uses Bricolage Grotesque and JetBrains Mono as web stand-ins. The Mac app will use SF Pro Rounded (heavy) for display and SF Mono for the caps labels.
